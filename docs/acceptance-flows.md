# Acceptance flows

1. An AI professor or lab reviewer opens the home page at 360px or 1440px, understands the applicant's research direction and academic fit, and can reach the CV in one interaction.
2. A reviewer reads a selected research or engineering case and finds the problem, contribution, result, and any approved repository link without opening a private source.
3. A reader opens `/resume.html`, prints it to PDF, and sees a readable two-page English A4 CV with no navigation controls.
4. A content editor changes only approved public content, runs quality checks, and confirms no vault token or private source enters the build.
