# Portfolio design system

## Direction

Research dossier: a persistent dark-navy identity rail paired with a white editorial reading surface, near-black serif display typography, thin technical rules, and evidence-rich research rows. The first screen must establish academic fit before showing breadth. Avoid cards, gradients, badges, fake metrics, and dense dashboard styling.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#FFFFFF`; ink: `#121A26`; muted: `#5B6674`; rule: `#DBE2EA`
- Sidebar navy: `#173D5A`; interactive blue: `#1A5B8C`; inverse text: `#F7FBFF`
- Display: Georgia / Noto Serif KR fallback; UI: Inter / Pretendard / system fallback
- Desktop: 300px identity rail plus fluid reading column; mobile gutter: 24px; touch target: 44px minimum

## Responsive QA

At 360px, the identity rail becomes a compact top band and evidence fields stack in reading order. At 1440px, the rail remains visible and project evidence keeps a three-column hierarchy. Keyboard focus must be visible and every external link states its destination through text.
